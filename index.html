Who We Are

Unknotting Technologies Ltd was formed in 2009 and incorporated in 2010. We are a reputable company specializing in logistics, security services, supply and installation of ICT equipment, office furniture, and construction services.

We serve government agencies, corporate bodies, educational institutions, and private individuals across Nigeria with branches in Lagos, Abuja, Anambra, and Taraba states.
